# Everyone Who Ever Lived

One life at random from everyone who was ever born.

The page draws a single human being from all the births there have ever been, weighted by how many people were actually born in each age and place, and tells you what that life most likely looked like: the odds of the draw, the name, the faith, the work, the food, the roof, the diseases and famines of the time, and how the life ended. You can draw from everyone ever born, from the last hundred years, from before 1926, or from any span of years you pick (190,000 BCE to today). Each life you draw gets an imagined portrait, painted once by an image model and kept. It can also deal a hundred lives at once, rank the conditions of your own birth against everyone ever born, and print a certificate.

**Live page:** https://everyone-who-ever-lived.pages.dev/

Every life is computed in one file (`site/index.html`). The only thing about a life that leaves the page is its link, sent to the site's own server to paint the portrait; the server recomputes the same life from it. The same link always reproduces the same life, and the same portrait. The site keeps two anonymous totals, visits and lives drawn, shown at the bottom of the page, and cookieless page-view statistics.

## Layout

- `site/`: the page as published on Cloudflare Pages.
- `functions/api/count.js`: the counter, a Pages function over one row in a D1 table.
- `functions/api/portrait/[id].js`: the portrait. It recomputes the life from its id with `lib/model.js`, builds the prompt the page shows, asks FLUX.2 [klein] 4B on Cloudflare Workers AI for a 768x1024 picture, and keeps it in the KV namespace `ewel-portraits`. At most 120 new portraits a day (table `portrait_days` in the same D1 database), which keeps it inside the Workers AI free allocation.
- `lib/model.js`: generated by `publish.py` from the model section of `site/index.html`. Never edit it by hand.
- `wrangler.toml`: the bindings (D1, KV, Workers AI).
- `index.html` at the root: the old GitHub Pages address, which now forwards to the new one and keeps the `#` so old links open the same life.
- `publish.py`: copies the page from its working copy, rebuilds `lib/model.js`, and deploys with wrangler (`--model-only` skips the deploy, for `wrangler pages dev`).

## Model and sources

The demographic model, its tables, the assumptions and every source are documented in [SOURCES.md](SOURCES.md). The one assumption that moves everything, the birth rate of the deep past, is exposed as a slider on the page. Open the browser console and run `AHE.selfCheck()` to compare 20,000 draws against the model.

Inspired by [anyhumanever.com](https://anyhumanever.com/), which draws its lives from a much larger server-side model.
